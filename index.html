TRI — Official System Rules

1. Objective

Eliminate all 3 of your opponent’s Leaders. If a single move mutually destroys both players’ last remaining Leaders, the game is a draw.

2. The Triangle of Advantage

Square beats Diamond, Diamond beats Circle, Circle beats Square.

  • Advantage (e.g. Square vs Diamond): defender captured, attacker gets an optional bonus move with the same piece. No 3rd move. A disadvantage strike on the bonus move causes mutual annihilation.
  • Neutral (same vs same): defender captured, turn ends.
  • Disadvantage (e.g. Square vs Circle): both pieces are eliminated, turn ends.

Sliding pieces are blocked by any piece in their path. Leaping pieces may jump over any piece (friendly or enemy) and land on an open tile or an enemy tile.

4. Turn Structure

Each turn, either move/engage one piece, or perform a Tactical Swap: exchange positions with an adjacent friendly piece (orthogonal or diagonal). A swap consumes the full turn.

5. Promotion

Every piece's range from the table above is reduced by 1. The first time a piece reaches the opponent's home rank (the far edge of the board), it is promoted, permanently restoring its normal range - shown with a +1 on the piece.
