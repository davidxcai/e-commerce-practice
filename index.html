TRI — Official System Rules

1. Objective

Eliminate all 3 of your opponent’s Leaders. If a single move mutually destroys both players’ last remaining Leaders, the game is a draw.

2. The Triangle of Advantage

A beats B, B beats C, C beats A.

  • Advantage (e.g. A vs B): defender captured, attacker gets an optional bonus move with the same piece. No 3rd move. A disadvantage strike on the bonus move causes mutual annihilation.
  • Neutral (same vs same): defender captured, turn ends.
  • Disadvantage (e.g. A vs C): both pieces are eliminated, turn ends.

Sliding pieces are blocked by any piece in their path. Leaping pieces may jump over any piece (friendly or enemy) and land on an open tile or an enemy tile.

4. Turn Structure

Each turn, either move/engage one piece, or perform a Tactical Swap: exchange positions with an adjacent friendly piece (orthogonal or diagonal). A swap consumes the full turn.
